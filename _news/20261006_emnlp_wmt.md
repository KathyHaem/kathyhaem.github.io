I'll be presenting my internship paper from LILT, [LocQE: Principled Domain Adaptation for Localisation Quality Estimation by Leveraging Post-Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18720)
in the WMT poster session.
Excited to talk to more people about this work!

My co-author Adnan will be in the main conference, presenting our paper [Predicting Multilingual Classification and Translation Performance of LLMs with Cross-Lingual Alignment -- Is English Enough?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03446).
I started work on this project last year, but she really grew this idea into something bigger and cooler.

Plus, I was part of the [Shared Task on Multitask LLMs with Limited Resources](https://www2.statmt.org/wmt26/limited-resources-llm.html) in two ways:
As a co-organiser, I was responsible for the zero-shot baseline and for several analyses.
And as an advisor, I asked a group of students to try working on this task---they really exceeded my initial expectations and ended up submitting a very nice system.

See you in Budapest!
