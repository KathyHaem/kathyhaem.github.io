title: Co-organising WLCD Workshop at COLING
---

Together with colleagues from TUM and friends, we're starting a new workshop at COLING 2027: [What LLMs Cannot Do (WLCD)](https://what-llms-can-not-do.org/).
The goal is to keep track of what SoTA LLMs can do in comparison to humans, whether that's based on a human study, an expert-annotated benchmark, or on a task that many would consider trivial (think "r's in strawberry")
The [Call for Papers](https://what-llms-can-not-do.org/cfp) is out now, with a submission deadline in February.
In the meantime, though, you can also submit already-published papers to our survey table!
